01 About

I'm a senior software engineer on the platform & architecture team at AppFolio, with nearly a decade in the Salesforce ecosystem and the systems that orbit it. My specialty is integration architecture — designing the connective tissue between CRMs, ERPs, telephony, and cloud services so data moves where it needs to, when it needs to. These days most of my work is agentic engineering: designing agent-to-agent communications for our enterprise apps and building AI agents and skills that take on real production work.

I've architected real-time pipelines on AWS Lambda and SQS, run a MuleSoft on-prem platform through a zero-downtime upgrade, wired Salesforce to billing, support, and compliance systems, and built the observability to keep it all honest. I like integrations that don't wake anyone up at 2 a.m.

Beyond the code, I lead large-scale initiatives — platform migrations, observability overhauls, architecture that pays for itself — and mentor engineers from early-career through fellow seniors as an SME across Salesforce, MuleSoft, and AWS.

Off the clock you'll find me with my family, traveling, cooking, or learning something new.
